Business Name: GS Associations
Industry: Premium Real Estate & Loan Advisory
Location: Hyderabad, India (specifically Financial District, Gachibowli)

Business Overview:
GS Associations is a premium real estate advisory and consultancy firm that has been operating in Hyderabad since 2012. Founded by Rajesh Gupta, the business aims to make property buying honest, simple, and rewarding by acting as true advisors for property buyers rather than just commission-driven agents. They have helped over 15,000 families and investors navigate the dynamic property market of Hyderabad.

Core Services:
1. Property Sales & Purchase: Residential, commercial, and investment properties.
2. Property Investment Consultation: Data-driven advisory to build profitable portfolios.
3. Home Loan Advisory: Securing home loans at competitive rates through partnerships with leading banks.
4. Legal Assistance: Providing in-house legal verification and ensuring full RERA compliance.
5. NRI Property Services: Specialized services for Non-Resident Indians investing in India.

Brand Values & Mission:
- Transparency and honesty (zero hidden charges).
- Data-driven and technology-first approach (AI-powered property matching).
- Providing lifetime advisory support.
- Trust, expertise, and a seamless client experience from enquiry to registration.

Key Achievements:
- Over 1,200 properties sold and 15,000+ happy families.
- Over ₹2,400 Crores in real estate transacted.
- Over 12 years of excellence in the industry.

Target Audience:
First-time homebuyers, experienced real estate investors, and NRIs looking for premium, secure, and trustworthy real estate and loan services in Hyderabad.
